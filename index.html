How to use this in your room
  1. One person shares their screen. Everyone else follows along and listens.
  2. Optional: add names in Setup so the page tells you whose turn it is.
  3. Draw a card. The speaker thinks, then explains, then the group adds to it. The timer runs these three phases for you.
  4. Listeners use the checklist. Then flip the card to self-check against the back.
  5. Mark any card that felt muddy. Your list is ready to bring back to the whole class.
